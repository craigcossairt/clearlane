Spin absorbs spikes so Pulse can discharge steadily, which means less battery repowering. The FAQ lists its uses as peak shaving, load shifting, power factor correction, backup, voltage correction, capacity expansion and demand response, and says Torus Lasso schedules charge and discharge automatically from facility load, rate schedules and grid signals. (https://www.torus.co/torus-nova-pulse)
- [read-on-source] Torus Lasso is the software that controls every Torus Station. Torus says it 'analyzes and predicts facility demand and grid conditions' and orchestrates Spin and Pulse at the meter and across distribution asset networks. Claims: millisecond data refresh, sub-250ms dispatch, 99.9% uptime. Its ML models analyze facility load patterns, weather data and grid signals to predict conditions on both sides of the meter and adjust charge and discharge automatically. 'True DER orchestration' routes each event to the right technology. 'Outcome visibility' quantifies cost savings, backup and power quality, and shows utility operators VPP event and dispatch performance across the fleet. It connects to SCADA, DERMS and ADMS through an API library. It runs autonomously, contrasted with EMS software that only 'monitors and reports'. Access is role-based: facilities see their own site, utility operators see and control the whole fleet. (https://www.torus.co/torus-lasso)
- [read-on-source] Torus Overwatch is a service run by certified operators and analysts in the Torus Overwatch Command Center (TOC), using AI-powered tooling. It covers real-time monitoring, proprietary anomaly detection, predictive analytics, predictive maintenance and 24/7 response. Stated scale: millions of data points per system per day, with sub-second behavioral analysis. It watches rotor dynamics in Spin, voltage and thermal behavior in Pulse, Shield security events and physical access. For grid support, it checks that each station responds properly and on time to frequency or voltage variations, and operators step in when a station underperforms. Customers get performance dashboards and have nothing to do themselves. (https://www.torus.co/torus-overwatch)

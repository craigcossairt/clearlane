- [read-on-source] SB 132 (2025), Electric Utility Amendments, is now Utah Code 54-26 and took effect May 7, 2025. Service for large-scale requests (secondary sources say 100 MW or more within five years) must go through PSC-approved large-load contracts. The customer pays its incremental costs and posts financial security. The utility has about 90 days to study a request; if it cannot serve, the customer may contract with a large-scale generation provider or build a closed private system. (https://le.utah.gov/Session/2025/bills/enrolled/SB0132.pdf ; https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/utah-is-taking-a-different-approach-to-new-data-center-load ; https://law.justia.com/codes/utah/title-54/chapter-26/part-3/section-301)

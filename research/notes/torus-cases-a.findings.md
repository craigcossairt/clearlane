- [read-on-source] Torus Lasso, as Torus describes it: the software layer controlling every Torus Station. ML models use facility load patterns, weather and grid signals to predict conditions on both sides of the meter and adjust charge/discharge. It routes each event to Spin or Pulse, quantifies savings, backup and power quality value for customers, and shows utility operators VPP event and dispatch performance in real time.

Clearlane: a day planner for simulated Torus-style stations on Utah's grid

It reads the CAISO day-ahead price for PacifiCorp East, fills Pulse in the midday clear lane, stays off the road in the evening jam, and keeps Spin ready for Rocky Mountain Power's calls. Then it writes an hourly envelope.

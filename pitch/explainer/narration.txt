## traffic | Traffic report
[ribbon] This is Utah's traffic report for October 27: the day-ahead price for PacifiCorp East.
[clear] At midday, power is cheapest. The road is clear.
[jam] Then solar fades, and by 6pm the price is {peak_over_trough} the day's low. That is the jam.

## race | Race
[race] Now race one battery through that day, two ways.
[fixed-buy] Left, a battery on an off-peak rule, our baseline, not Torus practice, buys overnight at {fixed_rule_charge_usd_mwh}.
[fixed-sell] It sells in the evening jam at {race_discharge_usd_mwh}. Sell minus buy is worth {fixed_usd_mw_day}.
[cl-buy] Right, a simulated Torus-style station on Clearlane buys the same energy at midday, at {timetable_charge_usd_mwh}.
[cl-sell] Same sale. It is worth {clearlane_usd_mw_day}, {race_ratio} as much.
[value] That is value to Rocky Mountain Power's system at day-ahead prices, per MW per day. Higher is better.

## call | Call
[call] At {call_time_mt}, Rocky Mountain Power calls for {enrolled_kw} kW. This call is simulated.
[spin] Spin, the flywheel, answers. The whole call fits inside its discharge.
[pulse] Pulse, the battery, carries on, and the host meter stays under {host_ceiling_kw} kW while Spin refills.
[record] Torus's own 2025 report counts {rmp_events_2025} events, all unplanned calls. That record stays separate from our simulation.

## replan | Re-plan
[replan] Then the rest of the day is solved again, from the state of charge the call left.
[holds] Spin carried the call, so Pulse keeps its plan.

## envelope | Envelope
[envelope] The output is an hourly envelope: reserve, and charge and discharge limits, for every hour.
[sch] On that day, {sch_hours_today} hours are System Critical, under a rule we propose. The envelope is a format Clearlane proposes.
